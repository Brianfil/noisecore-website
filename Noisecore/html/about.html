our mission and values

empowering music through innovation

Noisecore is committed to redefining how people experience and connect with sound. We believe music is not just heard — it's felt. Our mission is to empower creators and listeners with tools, technology, and freedom to explore sound in its purest form.

We value authenticity, elegance, creativity, and bold experimentation. Everything we build stems from our passion for helping others hear the world differently.

our history

2021
Noisecore was founded with a bold mission to revolutionize personal audio through innovation and design. Mr. Sung Jin Woo founded Noisecor while he was struggling to listen to his music in a crowded place.
2022
Launched our first line of noise-canceling headphones, NoiseCore Impact I — quickly gaining attention from the people.
2023
Expanded product lineup with wireless speakers and in-ear monitors, Noisecore Gaia, and Noisecore Clair — praised for sound clarity and build quality.
2024
Entered international markets and built a strong creator community around immersive audio culture.
2025
Recognized as the leading audio tech industry.
